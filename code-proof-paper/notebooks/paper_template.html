1. Abstract (150-250 words)
Problem statement, main results, and significance

Writing Guidelines for Abstract:

  • Start with the problem and its significance
  • State your main result precisely
  • Explain what makes this "code-backed" and why it matters
  • Mention key limitations
  • Keep it concise but complete

2. Introduction and Motivation
Context, motivation, and paper roadmap

Writing Guidelines for Introduction:

  • Set the mathematical/computational context
  • Explain why traditional proofs are challenging here
  • Describe how code helps with transparency
  • Outline your contributions
  • Provide a roadmap of the paper

3. Related Work
Prior work and how this differs

Writing Guidelines for Related Work:

  • Survey classical results in your area
  • Compare with proof assistants (Coq, Lean, Isabelle)
  • Contrast with property-based testing approaches
  • Highlight what makes your approach unique
