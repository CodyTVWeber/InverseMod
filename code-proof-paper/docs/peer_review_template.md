Peer Review Template

1. Summary of the paper in your own words (2–3 sentences).
2. Clarity: Are definitions and assumptions clear? Any ambiguous notation?
3. Correctness: Do claims logically follow? Any gaps?
4. Code-backed evidence: Are tests/properties sufficient and traceable?
5. Reproducibility: Do scripts run? Any environment issues?
6. Contribution and novelty: How does this advance the area?
7. Suggestions for improvement (specific and actionable).
8. Overall recommendation (accept/weak accept/weak reject/reject) and confidence.
